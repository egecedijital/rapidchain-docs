# Ecosystem

The Rapid Chain infrastructure is complete. We bring end-to-end Web3 tools for developers and users together under one roof. With our exchange **RapidSwap** at its heart, the full ecosystem is live at full capacity.

#### RapidSwap · [rapidswap.finance](https://rapidswap.finance)

**The new heart of liquidity on Rapid Chain.** Our next-generation exchange pairs proven, Uniswap-derived AMM infrastructure with built-in charting. Analyse the market instantly without an external tool, join liquidity pools and swap assets at high speed with low fees.

#### Rapid Order · [rapidorder.online](https://rapidorder.online)

**Beyond the AMM: the home of professional trading.** Rapid Order brings the central limit order book (CLOB) experience of centralised exchanges to the decentralised world. Orders match off-chain at high speed, while settlement is finalised directly on Rapid Chain through smart-contract escrow.

* Limit and market orders, a deep order book and live candlestick charts on the **RAPID/SETUSD** pair
* Deposit with Rapid Wallet, set your own price, verify every trade on Rapid Scan

#### Token Pilot · [tokenpilot.online](https://tokenpilot.online)

**Your own ERC-20, without writing a line of Solidity.** Create your token in the browser and deploy it straight from your own wallet; you are the sole owner of the contract. Pick only the features you need (mint, burn, max-wallet limit or transfer cooldown) and your contract is compiled accordingly.

#### RocketPad · [rocketpad.space](https://rocketpad.space)

**Launch a token in seconds.** A fast-launch platform built on Pump.fun-style dynamics for memecoins and project tokens. With its own built-in exchange and live chart screen, RocketPad unifies token creation and the first trade on a single screen.

#### Rapid Wallet · [wallet.rapidchain.io](https://wallet.rapidchain.io)

**Your secure gateway to the entire ecosystem.** Manage your assets, your tokens and every smart-contract interaction across dApps like Token Pilot on Rapid Chain, with full control.

#### Rapid Scan · [scan.rapidchain.io](https://scan.rapidchain.io)

**Absolute transparency.** Track every transfer, wallet movement, smart-contract approval and Token Pilot CREATE2 verification in real time on our native block explorer.

#### SETUSD & SETBridge · [setusd.com](https://setusd.com/)

**The stable foundation of the Rapid Chain economy.** SETUSD is the ecosystem's native stablecoin and the reference unit for all trading. The contract is built to OpenZeppelin v5 standards and secured by role-based access control and an emergency pause mechanism.

With **SETBridge**, every USDT locked on BNB Chain mints SETUSD **1:1** on Rapid Chain. Bring your assets over, trade SETUSD on Rapid Order and provide liquidity to RapidSwap pools. Hedging volatility or locking in profit no longer means leaving the network.
